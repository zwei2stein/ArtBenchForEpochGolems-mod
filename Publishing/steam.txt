[h1]Art Bench Support for Anima Bionics[/h1]

[h2]Features[/h2]

This mod patches the Anima Bionics mod to allow all recipes to also be crafted at the Art Bench.

[list]
    [*]Pawns can craft Anima bionics at the Art Bench.
    [*]No new buildings
    [*]No ballance changes
    [*]No new research
[/list]
