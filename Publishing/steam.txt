[h1]Art Bench Support for Epoch Golems[/h1]

[h2]Features[/h2]

This mod patches the [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3609646528]Epochs - Golems[/url] mod to allow crafting golems at the Art Bench

[list]
    [*]Can craft Golems at the Art Bench.
    [*]Golemancy board can be linked to Art Bench.
[/list]
